  • Legitimate interests: To operate, secure, and improve our Service where our interests are not overridden by your rights — for example, fraud prevention and analytics.

Your GDPR Rights

If you are located in the EEA, you have the right to:

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

How to Exercise Your GDPR Rights
