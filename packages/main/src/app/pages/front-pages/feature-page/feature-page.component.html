Build Courses, Faster

Create and launch engaging training in a day, not weeks. DoChek’s course builder feature lets you design modules, add MCQs or SCQs, and customize branding with your logo and colors. You can even export courses as SCORM packages to use across any eLearning platform.

Track Every Interaction

Traditional systems only tell you if a course is completed. DoChek’s AR/VR tracking captures every learner activity during immersive training sessions. Richer data helps you understand not just if training was done, but how it was experienced.

Keep Learners Engaged

Motivate employees with leaderboards, points, and scores that foster healthy competition. Built-in discussion forums make it easy to share ideas, reviews, and updates, just like a social feed, but for learning.

Our Story

Touchstone was founded on a simple yet powerful vision: to strengthen businesses by strengthening their people’s potential.

Dr. Frank Merritt, our Founder and CEO, harnessed his passion for psychology to pioneer the integration of behavioral science and technology for smart hiring and talent retention. Over time, this vision evolved, and today, Touchstone is dedicated exclusively to creating innovative eLearning and consulting solutions.

Our strategic approach has built a rich legacy of digital learning innovation that now supports Fortune 500 clients globally. Our team spans two continents, united by a common purpose to empower organizations to thrive in an ever-changing world.
